Hey, I'm Kai Zhou.

I'm a developer curious about artificial intelligence, software engineering, and product design.

I build with <a href="https://www.python.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="markdown-magic-link whitespace-nowrap"><span aria-hidden="true" class="i-simple-icons-python text-[#3776ab] dark:text-[#79b4df]" /> Python</a> and <a href="https://www.typescriptlang.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="markdown-magic-link whitespace-nowrap"><span aria-hidden="true" class="i-simple-icons-typescript text-[#3178c6] dark:text-[#78b4ed]" /> TypeScript</a>, and use <a href="https://claude.ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="markdown-magic-link whitespace-nowrap"><span aria-hidden="true" class="i-simple-icons-claude text-[#c15f3c] dark:text-[#e5a087]" /> Claude</a>, <a href="https://openai.com/codex/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="markdown-magic-link whitespace-nowrap"><span aria-hidden="true" class="i-simple-icons-openai text-[#555] dark:text-[#ddd]" /> Codex</a>, and <a href="https://chatgpt.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="markdown-magic-link whitespace-nowrap"><span aria-hidden="true" class="i-simple-icons-openai text-[#16846b] dark:text-[#74c9ae]" /> ChatGPT</a> to explore ideas and work through problems. My interests span LLMs, agents, and the tools that make them useful.

I enjoy breaking down complex problems and turning ideas into tools people can actually use. This site is where I share the [projects](/projects) I'm working on, the [articles](/posts) I write, and ideas I pick up along the way. You can also find the [tools and setup](/use) I use every day.

Lately, I've been exploring AI applications, open-source tools, and more natural ways for people to work with computers. Rather than chasing every new buzzword, I care more about whether a tool is clear, reliable, and genuinely reduces repetitive work.

When I'm not coding, I read, take photos, or wander somewhere I haven't been before. Some of those moments end up in my [photos](/photos).
